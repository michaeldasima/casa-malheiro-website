Lourear a Pevide 2026: two months of Loureiro Vinho Verde and small plates in Ponte de Lima

5 September 2026 · Casa Malheiro

From early October to the end of November, Ponte de Lima becomes a relaxed route for anyone interested in local wine and food. According to the official tourism page, Lourear a Pevide 2026 runs from 1 October to 30 November 2026 and pairs Ponte de Lima’s Loureiro Vinho Verde with traditional and creative small dishes.

The format is easy to enjoy during a short autumn stay. There are 20 participating venues across the municipality, including restaurants, cafés and traditional taverns. Some are in the historic centre, while others are in the surrounding parishes. The idea is simple: a glass of Ponte de Lima Loureiro Vinho Verde with a special snack.

For visitors, that makes the event useful rather than demanding. You can walk by the Lima river, spend time in the old centre, choose one stop for tasting and keep the rest of the day open. It is not the kind of event that forces your whole trip around one evening.

Casa Malheiro, in Freixo near Ponte de Lima, fits this kind of slow autumn plan. The holiday homes offer space for families or groups, kitchens, parking and a calm base for exploring Ponte de Lima, Viana do Castelo, Braga and the wider Minho region. After an afternoon of food, wine and a short drive, it is good to return somewhere quiet.

A simple itinerary could be: one day in Ponte de Lima and its historic centre, one day exploring nearby villages or natural areas, and one Lourear a Pevide stop along the way. It is an easy way to taste the region without overloading the holiday.

If you would like to combine Lourear a Pevide with a few calm days in the Minho, Casa Malheiro’s holiday homes make a practical base near Ponte de Lima. Send us the dates you have in mind.
