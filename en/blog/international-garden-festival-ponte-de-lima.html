A summer day at the International Garden Festival in Ponte de Lima

13 June 2026 · Casa Malheiro

A good holiday day does not always need a big plan. Sometimes a walk, a bit of shade, a few unexpected details and a slow evening afterwards are enough.

That is why the International Garden Festival in Ponte de Lima works so well as a summer outing. The 21st edition runs from 29 May to 31 October 2026 and follows the theme “Gardens of Dreams”. Each year, the festival presents temporary garden installations that bring together nature, design and different ways of imagining green spaces.

For guests visiting Northern Portugal, it is a good reason to spend a few unhurried hours in Ponte de Lima. You do not need to be a garden expert. Families, couples and travellers looking for a quieter day can simply walk around, notice the details and enjoy the setting.

Ponte de Lima makes the rest of the day easy too. After visiting the festival, there is still time for a walk by the Lima river, a coffee in the centre or a short stroll through the old town. It is better not to overfill the day. The appeal is in taking it slowly.

Casa Malheiro as a calm base

From Casa Malheiro, this kind of outing pairs naturally with a calm evening back in Freixo. You can head to Ponte de Lima in the morning or early afternoon, then return later to cook, barbecue, read or rest. In summer, that balance is often just right: a local trip, without the feeling that the whole day has been spent on the road.

The festival is also a good option for families or multi-generation groups. Not every day has to mean the beach, a city visit or a long drive. Sometimes a nearby place with something gentle to explore is enough.

Practical note

If you are staying in the region during summer or early autumn, the International Garden Festival is worth considering as a half-day trip. Before visiting, check the official website for current opening times and practical information.
