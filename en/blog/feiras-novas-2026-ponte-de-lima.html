Feiras Novas 2026 in Ponte de Lima: September festival days

25 July 2026 · Casa Malheiro

From 9 to 14 September 2026, Ponte de Lima will celebrate Feiras Novas. It is one of the town’s most important annual moments and brings many visitors to the historic centre.

The Municipality of Ponte de Lima describes Feiras Novas as “the largest live congress of popular culture in Portugal”. The festival is held in honour of Nossa Senhora das Dores and is recognised as National Intangible Cultural Heritage.

During these days, Ponte de Lima becomes much livelier than usual: concertinas, traditional singing, folklore groups, food, music and people in the streets from day into evening. For travellers who want to see the Minho at a festive time of year, September is a good moment to visit.

A simple plan for the festival days

From Casa Malheiro, the plan can stay easy: spend a quiet morning at the house, visit Ponte de Lima later in the day, enjoy the festival atmosphere and return to the calm of the holiday home afterwards.

If you would like to combine Feiras Novas with quieter days in the Minho countryside, Casa Malheiro is a practical base near Ponte de Lima.
