Feira do Cavalo in Ponte de Lima: a July weekend of horses, tradition and Minho life

30 May 2026 · Casa Malheiro

At the beginning of July, Ponte de Lima becomes closely linked to the world of horses for a few days. From 2 to 5 July 2026, the XVIII Feira do Cavalo is planned at Expolima — an event that brings together equestrian culture, local tradition and the everyday life of the Minho region.

For visitors, it is more than a horse fair. Around the venue you find riders, families, breeders, spectators and people from the area. There are presentations, competitions, encounters and the kind of summer atmosphere Ponte de Lima does well: long evenings, walks by the Lima River and a slower pace than in the larger cities.

Why it is worth making a weekend of it

If you come for only a few hours, you will probably see part of the event. If you stay for two or three nights, it becomes a small Minho break.

  • arrive calmly and walk through Ponte de Lima in the evening
  • set aside one day for the Feira do Cavalo and Expolima
  • use the next morning for the town, the river or a short trip nearby
  • leave enough free time, especially when travelling with children or several generations

Events like this are easier to enjoy when everything is not squeezed into one day. Ponte de Lima is compact enough for a relaxed visit, while the surrounding region gives you good reasons to stay a little longer.

For families and horse lovers

The Feira do Cavalo works well for families who want to show children something local, not only beaches or swimming pools. Horses, demonstrations, people from the region, food and time outdoors are easy to understand and often more accessible for children than a formal cultural visit.

Even if you do not ride yourself, the event offers a simple way to experience regional culture. In the Minho, fairs, local festivals and agricultural traditions are still close to everyday life. That is what makes dates like this interesting for guests: you see more than a landmark; you get a feeling for how the region lives.

Casa Malheiro as a calm base

Our holiday homes in Freixo are a practical base if you want to visit Ponte de Lima and the surrounding area without the routine of a hotel. After a day at the Feira do Cavalo, it helps to have your own kitchen, more space, a garden and a quiet evening at the house — especially with children or when several adults are travelling together.

From Casa Malheiro, you can combine Ponte de Lima with other day trips: Viana do Castelo, Braga, small Minho villages or simply a free afternoon by the pool. In that way, a visit to one event becomes a few real days in northern Portugal.

Practical note

The Feira do Cavalo takes place at Expolima in Ponte de Lima. As event programmes and times can change, it is worth checking the official event and municipality pages before travelling.

If you are thinking about visiting the Minho in early July, this weekend is a good reason to plan your dates early, especially for families who would like to stay several nights.
